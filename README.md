# Fine Jewelry Calculators & Gemological Tools Suite

An open-source collection of algorithmic calculation models for fine jewelry valuation, diamond pricing, and precious metal purity verification. Developed by the engineering team at [Oshinic Jewels](https://oshinicjewels.com).

---

## 💎 Live Interactive Web Applications

You can use the live, zero-friction interactive versions of these tools on our website:

* **[Diamond Price Estimator](https://oshinicjewels.com/tools/diamond-price-estimator):** Interactive 4Cs valuation (Cut, Color, Clarity, Carat) comparing natural vs. lab-grown pricing curves.
* **[Gold Purity & Melt Calculator](https://oshinicjewels.com/tools/gold-purity-calculator):** Calculates fine gold grams across 10K, 14K, 18K, 22K, and 24K and estimates live scrap melt value per troy ounce.
* **[Ring Size Calculator & Converter](https://oshinicjewels.com/tools/ring-size-calculator):** Precision millimeter-to-scale sizing converter with US, UK, EU, and Asian ring sizes.
* **[Zodiac & Birthstone Finder](https://oshinicjewels.com/tools/birthstone-finder):** Mineralogical and astrological gemstone database.
* **[Precious Metal Comparison Matrix](https://oshinicjewels.com/tools/metal-comparison):** Density, hardness, and durability comparison for Platinum, Gold, Titanium, and Silver.
* **[Explore All 12 Free Tools](https://oshinicjewels.com/tools)**
